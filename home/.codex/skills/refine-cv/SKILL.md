# Refine a CV

Read the current CV, supplied experience notes and any target job description. Identify the authoritative editable source before changing exports. Use the applicant’s latest corrections over older documents. Keep verified facts, proposed phrasing and unresolved details distinct.

## Evidence and wording

Write one main idea per bullet: action, relevant task or scope, then a supported outcome or purpose. Distinguish a contribution from sole ownership and a research recommendation from an implemented result. Quantify only supplied or verified quantities; scope such as banks compared or participants recruited can be useful without a business-impact metric.

Aim for one or two rendered lines. Roughly 12–20 words is a useful editing target for ordinary bullets, with shorter or longer wording when it preserves meaning. This word range is an editorial default, not an empirically established optimum. Prefer a direct verb phrase to a sentence with a personal pronoun. Preserve distinctions such as designed, contributed, coordinated and moderated.

For a profile, try two short sentences, usually around 15–25 words each: relevant experience and specialism, then evidence or domain scope. Trim repetition before shrinking type. Check actual line breaks rather than enforcing a word count mechanically.

Represent tools through demonstrated applications and accurate proficiency. For AI experience, distinguish using Claude, ChatGPT and agent workflows from building agents or integrating APIs. Name the tools and evidenced uses, such as synthesis or reporting; technical development claims need their own evidence. Preserve basic or introductory proficiency in analysis tools.

Keep degree completion status explicit. Preserve the supplied qualification title until an official translation is verified. Use relevant coursework without implying successful completion of every module or current enrolment. Grades and exchanges are contextual choices; explain unfamiliar grades accurately rather than upgrading their meaning.

## Order and relevance

Choose section order by the strongest evidence for the role. For an established professional whose experience is more relevant than their qualification, a useful default is Profile → Work Experience → Skills → Education. Recent graduates and qualification-led roles may warrant Education earlier. Keep entries within each history in reverse chronology.

Order skill groups by the vacancy’s priorities. Research methods, languages, analytical tools and AI workflows can change order depending on interview language, technical demands and the role. Maintain proficiency labels and avoid repeating the same tool in multiple groups. Treat routine digital tools as lower priority when they add little evidence for the target role.

Read [the source notes](references/guidance.md) when justifying section order, bullet length or typography, or when adapting guidance between countries. The sources are guidance with a context, not universal hiring rules.

## Typography and exports

Use one conventional, actually available family with real regular and bold faces. Check font resolution in the export environment: a requested family can silently resolve to another. Start around 11 pt for body text, with 10.5 pt a reasonable compact option; keep important metadata readable, usually 10 pt or more. A 24 pt bold name, 10.5–11 pt bold headings and approximately 1.2 body leading are practical starting points rather than mandatory dimensions.

Use 400/700 weights when the family supplies regular and bold faces. Maintain comfortable spacing and restrained contrast. Improve selection, wrapping and spacing before reducing font size. Choose page count for the market, role and application instructions; a one-page or two-page target should not override readable content.

Keep contact labels readable and clickable. Apply the user’s chosen contact grouping across formats. Decorative icons should accompany text; use vector assets or embedded images when font symbols harm portability or extraction. Retain exact hyperlink destinations and the requested address granularity.

When exporting, inspect the actual PDF page image and confirm selectable text, dates, diacritics, page breaks, links and embedded font families. Check the editable Word styles and hyperlinks separately; PDF acceptance does not establish pagination in every recipient’s Word installation. Run the project’s existing relevant export checks. Report the resulting files and any material unverified behavior.
