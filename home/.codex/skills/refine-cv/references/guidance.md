# Source notes for CV editing

Reviewed 19 October 2026. Recheck links and local application requirements when they affect a new decision. These sources support principles; exact word counts, sizes and section orders in the skill are context-sensitive editing defaults.

- [MIT CAPD — Writing about your skills](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/resumes-writing-about-your-skills/): action, project/context and result; quantitative scope can demonstrate scale; statements should normally fit one or two lines. The source does not establish one optimal word count.
- [Harvard MCS — Resume template with bullet points](https://cdn-careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/161/2024/07/resume-bullets.pdf): action verbs, relevant accomplishments and supported quantification; verb phrases without personal pronouns. The template is not a universal German layout specification.
- [Oxford University — CVs](https://www.ox.ac.uk/careers/careers-guidance/job-search-and-applications/writing-applications/cvs): concise, relevant evidence; one idea per bullet; experienced research staff may put experience above education. Its examples address an academic/university audience, so apply their principles to the target role rather than copying the sequence blindly.
- [FAU career service — Der Lebenslauf](https://www.career.rw.fau.de/files/2022/07/Lebenslauf.pdf): consistent formatting, readable sans-serif type, restrained colors, and important information first. It gives Calibri and Arial as examples and does not identify a uniquely best family.
- [Bundesagentur für Arbeit — Bewerbungskompass für Arbeitsstellen](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/datei/bewerbungskompass-arbeitsstellen_ba091697.pdf): recommends a consistent font family and 10–12 pt, with conventional font examples. Check the actual PDF for context; this is guidance, not evidence that one family improves selection rates.

## Rendering choices

Liberation Sans is a useful local option when it resolves to genuine installed font files and regular/bold faces. It is not an official requirement. Arial, Calibri or another conventional family can be preferable when the actual export environment and recipient have those fonts. Verify rather than infer substitution or embedding from a CSS declaration or Word style name.

Do not describe the resulting CV as guaranteed to pass an applicant tracking system. Text and link extraction checks establish observable document properties, not compatibility with every hiring system.
